---
name: Mobile SEO content disclosures
description: Project convention for long supplementary SEO and help content on mobile.
---

Keep long supplementary SEO/help copy in the page and reveal it on mobile through an accessible “More” disclosure. Leave it expanded on larger screens. Keep primary instructions and prose-first pages, such as guides and legal pages, visible.

**Why:** The user chose a shorter mobile experience without removing the supporting content, and mobile-first search guidance allows equivalent content in accessible accordions.

**How to apply:** Use this for secondary long-form content on game pages. Do not collapse the information needed to complete the current task or content pages whose main purpose is reading.
